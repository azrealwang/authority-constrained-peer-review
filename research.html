Research agenda

From a position
to a program of research.

Six objectives for making authority-constrained peer review practical, testable and open to scrutiny.

A shared agenda, not a closed project plan.

These objectives describe what is needed to advance the vision. They need not all be completed by the project collaborators. Relevant work from the wider community may also be featured as the project develops.

The six objectives

01Foundations

Develop structured scrutiny

Make the conditions for decision influence explicit.

How can evidential support, interpretive validity and decision proportionality be operationalized across submissions, reviewers and fields?

Research questions & opportunities ＋
  • Map concrete review failure modes to the three scrutiny dimensions.
  • Distinguish legitimate differences in interpretation from unsupported claims.
  • Study when a valid criticism warrants the decision influence assigned to it.

Potential contributions

Operational definitions, scrutiny protocols and studies of consistency and usefulness.

Relevant perspectives

Metascience, scientific evaluation, institutional governance and domain expertise.

Discuss this objective ↗
02Foundations

Establish datasets for structured scrutiny

Preserve disagreement, provenance and uncertainty.

What data would let us study consequential review judgments, their scrutiny and subsequent adjudication without assuming that existing judgments are ground truth?

Research questions & opportunities ＋
  • Connect submissions, human and AI critiques, ratings, scrutiny signals and adjudication.
  • Preserve the source of each judgment and meaningful disagreement.
  • Validate whether synthetic problematic reviews exhibit the intended failure mode.

Potential contributions

Annotation frameworks, research datasets and evaluation protocols.

Relevant perspectives

Dataset design, expert annotation, uncertainty modeling and research ethics.

Discuss this objective ↗
03Methods

Develop AI supervision methods

Examine the judgment as well as the submission.

How can AI help identify review problems and inconsistencies between written reasoning and consequential ratings?

Research questions & opportunities ＋
  • Study rating–reasoning consistency using the review and relevant submission content.
  • Identify unsupported claims, omissions and interpretive problems.
  • Evaluate the usefulness of counter-signals for human adjudication.

Potential contributions

Supervision methods and evaluations of scrutiny signals.

Relevant perspectives

NLP, LLM evaluation, review analysis and human-in-the-loop systems.

Discuss this objective ↗
04Methods

Develop less-biased and more reliable AI review support

Study critique quality and its downstream effects.

How can AI-generated critique provide useful evidence while limiting systematic bias and excessive anchoring of human supervisors?

Research questions & opportunities ＋
  • Improve factual grounding and the reliability of substantive critiques.
  • Measure evaluator-specific tendencies and systematic biases.
  • Test how critique length, structure and issue selection affect human judgment.

Potential contributions

Critique-generation methods and human–AI interaction studies.

Relevant perspectives

LLM methods, bias measurement, HCI and experimental design.

Discuss this objective ↗
05Security

Address technical misconduct

Protect both critique and scrutiny from manipulation.

How can review systems resist attempts to manipulate AI-generated critiques or AI scrutiny of human judgments?

Research questions & opportunities ＋
  • Evaluate prompt injection, adversarial text edits, data poisoning and implicit manipulation.
  • Test safeguards in peer-review-specific threat models.
  • Study the trade-off between procedural transparency and exposure to manipulation.

Potential contributions

Threat models, security evaluations and auditable safeguards.

Relevant perspectives

AI security, adversarial testing, system design and auditability.

Discuss this objective ↗
06Deployment

Pilot authority-constrained review

Learn from controlled use in real review settings.

How can these principles be tested in existing workflows while measuring workload, disagreement resolution and decision influence?

Research questions & opportunities ＋
  • Begin with scrutiny of existing human reviews and human adjudication.
  • Evaluate full mutual-supervision workflows where appropriate.
  • Measure reviewer and chair workload, anchoring, contestability and robustness.

Potential contributions

Controlled studies, deployment protocols and venue-level pilot evidence.

Relevant perspectives

Conference and journal organizers, program committees, review platforms and empirical researchers.

Discuss this objective ↗
